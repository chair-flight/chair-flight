## Subject guidelines

### 010 Air Law

- Cite ICAO documents down to the paragraph, for example `### ICAO Doc 8168, Volume I, Part II, Section 5, Chapter 7, 7.1.7`. Cite EU law as `### SERA.8020(b), Adherence to flight plan` or `### Regulation (EU) 2017/373, ATS.TR.320`.
- Doc 8168 Volume I: Part II, Section 5, Chapter 5 is precision approach (5.7.6.2 half-scale deflection), Chapter 6 is circling and Chapter 7 is missed approach; Part II, Section 6 is holding (2.2.x covers hold entry). It has no altimeter-setting text: use Doc 4444, 4.10.
- Doc 8168 Volume II: Part I, Section 2, Chapter 2, Table I-2-2-3 gives the fix tolerance for TAR and RSR; Part II, Section 1, Chapter 1, 1.1.3 gives the wheel-to-glide-path-antenna distances (aeroplane categories A/B 6 m, C/D 7 m, H 3 m, DL 8 m).
- The default turn direction for non-standard holds ("right turns") is not in Doc 8168 Part II, Section 6: do not quote it from there.
- Doc 4444, 8.5.5.1.1 gives the tolerance for pressure-altitude-derived level information displayed to the controller: plus or minus 60 m (200 ft) in RVSM airspace, plus or minus 90 m (300 ft) elsewhere. 8.5.5.1.2 and following cover verification and the 90 m (300 ft) level-crossing rule.
- Doc 4444 and SERA carry the ATS rules; Regulation (EU) 2017/373 carries the ATS provider requirements (for example ATS.TR.320(a)(2): immediate ATIS update after a significant change).
- Annex 14 is reached through Regulation (EU) No 139/2014 and EASA's CS ADR-DSN for the EU. 139/2014, Article 2 defines a wet runway and standing water (3 mm threshold). AMC1 ADR.OPS.B.037 applies the 25 % coverage threshold to each runway third. CS ADR-DSN.M.710 specifies green taxiway centre line lights with runway proximity and exit exceptions.
- Annex 15 (`annex-15.txt`) covers definitions, AIP structure and NOTAM. The copy does not contain the AIC and ASHTAM colour-code pages.
- SERA covers converging aircraft (SERA.3210), special VFR (SERA.5005, SERA.5010), initial RMZ calls (SERA.6005), channel digits (SERA.14035), and radio failure and blind transmission (SERA.14083, SERA.14085): a blind message is transmitted twice on the designated channels after unsuccessful contact attempts.
- Regulation (EU) No 2018/1139 never says "hard law" or "soft law": those are EASA's terms. Do not present a Basic Regulation quote as saying so.
- Part-FCL FCL.020(b) sets the first solo age at 16. Chicago Convention Articles 32 and 33 set the conditions for recognising flight crew licences. Regulation (EC) No 300/2008, Article 1 sets the common rules against unlawful interference and a shared basis for interpreting Annex 17.
- AMC1 FCL.210; FCL.215 (Aircrew Easy Access Rules, pages 234–235) carries the radio-navigation syllabus onto page 235: DME range and accuracy, radar and GNSS.

### 070 Operational procedures

- All of Part-ORO, Part-CAT and their AMC/GM are in `easa/easy-access-rules-air-operations-965-2012.txt` (full 2,566 pages; an earlier copy was truncated). Entries marked "applicable until/from 2028" exist in two versions: quote the one in force.
- Operations manual: ORO.MLR.100 (access), ORO.MLR.101 (Parts A to D), AMC3 ORO.MLR.100 (contents by part). MEL: ORO.MLR.105 and CAT.IDE.A.105. Record keeping: ORO.MLR.115.
- Ground icing: CAT.OP.MPA.250 with GM1 (definitions), GM2 (checks) and GM3 (fluid types, hold-over time).
- Oxygen: CAT.IDE.A.235 (pressurised), CAT.IDE.A.240 (non-pressurised), CAT.IDE.A.230 (first-aid), CAT.IDE.A.245 (PBE), CAT.OP.MPA.285 (use by flight crew).
- Management system and FDM: ORO.GEN.200, AMC1 ORO.GEN.200(a)(1), ORO.AOC.130 with its AMC. Task specialists: SPO.GEN.106.
- Questions that need SERA, SPA.RVSM/MNPS, Annex 6 attachments or the Doc 4444 route-reporting text were left unexplained until that text is quoted.

### 031–033 Mass, performance and flight planning

- Extra fuel (CAT) is the smallest of three limits: RTOM − AZFM − (block − taxi); MLM − AZFM − (block − taxi − trip); tank capacity − block. For a climb to the optimum altitude, use min(RTOM, mass restriction).
- Trip fuel change: when the NAM is given, the TAS is irrelevant and only fuel per NAM changes (+0.5 % per 10 °C). When the NGM is given, air distance = ground distance × TAS / GS.
- Calculations use the CAP 697, CAP 698 and Jeppesen extracts referenced in the question's annexes; quote the table or figure number in the explanation.
